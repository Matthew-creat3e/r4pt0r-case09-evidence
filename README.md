# Executive Summary

This dossier consolidates the findings generated during the recovery of the IVAN Recovery Environment following a sequence of incidents involving the known threat actors Wobblebottom, Fiddlestump, Nibblenog, and Grumblewort.

The purpose of this record is to provide a complete audit trail of recovery operations, document the restoration of affected subsystems, catalogue investigative findings, and establish whether sufficient evidence exists to support final recovery attestation.

The review incorporates evidence generated during all completed mission phases and reflects the current operational status of the IVAN Recovery Environment.

# Threat Actor Summary

## Wobblebottom

Primary activity involved unauthorized account usage and manipulation of identity records.

Observed behaviors included:

- Introduction of suspicious account data.
- Improper use of authentication mechanisms.
- Attempts to exploit assumptions regarding account ownership.
- Abuse of trust relationships associated with session identity.

Recovery actions focused on authentication review, account validation, and restoration of trusted identity records.

Status: RESOLVED

---

## Fiddlestump

Primary activity involved concealment of information and interference with recovery processes.

Observed behaviors included:

- Obfuscation of critical cryptographic information.
- Scrambling and concealment of communications.
- Interference with memory restoration operations.

Recovery actions included key reconstruction, forensic analysis of communications, and restoration of archived information.

Status: RESOLVED

---

## Nibblenog

Primary activity involved unsafe application functionality.

Observed behaviors included:

- Hidden application interfaces.
- Unsafe evaluation of user-supplied input.
- Failure to properly separate code from data.
- Exposure of application logic to manipulation.

Recovery actions included secure code review, identification of undocumented functionality, and implementation of secure processing controls.

Status: RESOLVED

---

## Grumblewort

Primary activity involved manipulation of trust relationships and authority structures.

Observed behaviors included:

- Unverified operational requests.
- Abuse of maintenance workflows.
- Reliance upon untrusted information.
- Attempts to influence decision making using unsupported claims.

Recovery actions focused on authorization review, trust validation, evidence evaluation, and verification procedures.

Status: RESOLVED
