### Executive Summary

This record documents the final review of recovery operations conducted following the compromise of the IVAN Recovery Environment.

Recovery activities were initiated after multiple unauthorized modifications were traced to known threat actors operating under the aliases:

- Wobblebottom
- Fiddlestump
- Nibblenog
- Grumblewort

Corrective investigations were conducted to identify affected systems, validate recovery actions, and restore normal operation.
